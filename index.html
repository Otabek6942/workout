Push-ups

1

Starting Position

Get into a plank position with your hands slightly wider than your shoulders. Keep your body in a straight line from head to heels.

2

Lowering Phase

Bend your elbows and lower your body until your chest nearly touches the floor. Keep your elbows at a 45-degree angle.

3

Pushing Phase

Push through your hands to return to the starting position. Keep your core engaged throughout the movement.

Tips for Proper Form

  • Keep your core tight throughout the movement
  • Maintain a straight line from head to heels
  • Breathe in as you lower, breathe out as you push up
  • Keep your elbows at a 45-degree angle
  • Don't let your hips sag or rise
